## 1. Introducing MotorPal (`01-introducing.png`)

Hello, LinkedIn. We are MotorPal.

Owning a vehicle in Sri Lanka means keeping track of a lot: emission tests, insurance, the revenue licence, oil changes, tyres, batteries, warranties and fuel costs. Most of it lives in a glovebox, a phone gallery or someone's memory.

MotorPal brings it into one place. It reminds you before deadlines, keeps every service record, shows what your fuel really costs, and explains things in plain words, so you always know your vehicle is in good shape.

We are building it now. Follow the page to see it come together.

## 2. The problem (`02-the-problem.png`)

Quick question for every vehicle owner: where is your service history right now?

For most of us it is spread across a glovebox, a WhatsApp chat and a guess ("around April?"). That is how deadlines get missed and how a car with a good history ends up with no proof of it when it is time to sell.

We think owners deserve better, and that is the problem MotorPal is solving.

Where do you keep yours? Tell us in the comments.

#MotorPal #SriLanka #CarCare #ProductDevelopment

## 3. Four pillars (`03-four-pillars.png`)

Our logo is a gauge made of four segments. Each one is a job MotorPal does for you:

1. Reminders: emission test, insurance and revenue licence, in the order Sri Lanka requires them.
2. Service records: every oil change and part, with the date, kilometres and garage.
3. Fuel and costs: your km per litre, and what each kilometre really costs.
4. Warranties: battery, tyres and parts, with the date each one runs out.

Four pillars, one app.

## 4. A first look (`04-look-inside.png`)

A first look inside MotorPal.

Home opens with Car health: which parts are due, in kilometres and days. Below that sits what is coming up, from the emission test to your insurance renewal.

It is built for Sri Lanka. It knows the emission test, insurance and revenue licence order, works offline with your records kept on your phone, and Sinhala and Tamil are on the way.

We are looking for early testers. If you own a car, van, motorbike or three-wheeler and would like to try it, send us a message.

## 5. Why it matters (`05-why-it-matters.png`)

More than 2,700 people lost their lives on Sri Lankan roads in 2025 (Sri Lanka Police figures, as reported in the press).

Most crashes have many causes, and no app can prevent them. But a well-kept vehicle removes one avoidable risk for the people you carry, and with regular roadworthiness testing on the way, keeping it in good shape matters more than ever.

MotorPal helps owners do exactly that, and keeps the proof in one place.

Follow MotorPal for the launch.
