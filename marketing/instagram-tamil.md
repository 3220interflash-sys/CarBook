# Instagram: Tamil set, "CarBook Cinema" (draft)

Its own theme, not a translation. Each post is a classic Tamil cinema scene: hero entry, punch dialogue, interval twist, comedy track, the bill-shock scene, the rain song, the silent villain, heroine as hero, the trilogy, the BGM.

Rules for this set:
- Parody the tropes and well-known phrases only. No actor names, photos, film titles, posters or music.
- Sri Lankan Tamil usage (காப்புறுதி for insurance, வருமான அனுமதிப்பத்திரம் for revenue licence). Places: Wellawatte, Jaffna, Batticaloa, Trinco, Negombo.
- A native Sri Lankan Tamil speaker must check every line before posting.

Images: `marketing/instagram/posts-ta/`. Text: `marketing/instagram/posts-ta.js`. Redraw with `node marketing/instagram/make.js ta`.
