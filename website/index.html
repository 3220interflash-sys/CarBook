At the garage

Never be put on the spot again.

The mechanic says something you don't quite follow, and you're expected to agree on the spot. Here's what some common phrases usually mean, and what you can ask back.

"Your brake pads are worn."

Usually means: the pads that press on the brakes are getting thin and will need replacing.

Ask: How thick are they now, and how many kilometres are left?

"You need a wheel alignment."

Usually means: the wheels aren't pointing quite straight, so tyres wear unevenly.

Ask: What did you see that shows it's off? Is there uneven tyre wear?

"We should flush the coolant."

Usually means: draining and replacing the liquid that keeps the engine cool.

Ask: Is it due by my manual, or is something wrong with the old coolant?

"Let's do a scan."

Usually means: plugging in a reader to see the fault codes the car has stored.

Ask: What code came up, and can you show or write it down for me?

"Your shock absorbers are leaking."

Usually means: the parts that smooth out bumps are losing their oil and working less well.

Ask: Which ones, and can you show me the leak?

"Genuine or aftermarket?"

Usually means: a part made by your vehicle's maker, or a cheaper one from another company.

Ask: What's the price difference, and is there a warranty on each?

These are general explanations to help you ask better questions. Your mechanic, who can see your vehicle, has the final word. In the app, every part's page explains it in the same plain way, and Questions for my mechanic builds questions from your own records.

Questions

Good questions, straight answers.

Is CarBook really free?

Yes. Reminders, records, fuel tracking, the dashboard lights guide and the assistant are free for vehicle owners.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Open the app and add your vehicle. Your records are saved on your phone. Use Export in the app to keep a backup copy.

Which phones does it work on?

Any smartphone with a web browser. On Android, open it in Chrome and choose "Install app" or "Add to Home screen" to use it like a normal app. A Google Play version is on the way.

Is it in Sinhala and Tamil?

CarBook is in English today. Sinhala and Tamil versions are being written and checked by native speakers.

Does CarBook replace my mechanic?

No. CarBook explains things in plain words and suggests what to ask, but it never diagnoses. For anything that feels unsafe, especially brakes, tyres or a red warning light, see a professional.

Where do the service intervals come from?

CarBook uses common default intervals, such as an oil change every 5,000 km or 6 months, and always labels them as defaults. Your owner's manual comes first.

Can I renew my licence through CarBook?

Not directly. CarBook reminds you, shows what needs to be valid first, and keeps your renewal history. You renew through the official eRL service or your Divisional Secretariat as usual.

Is my information safe?

Your records stay on your phone and are never sent to us. Read our privacy policy for the details.
