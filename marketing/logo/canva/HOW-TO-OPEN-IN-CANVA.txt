MotorPal logo for Canva

1. Open canva.com and sign in.
2. Click Create a design > Import file (or drag a file onto the Canva home page).
3. Pick one of the PDF files below. Canva opens it as a design you can edit:
   change colours, move or resize parts, change the text.
   - motorpal-icon.pdf            the app icon (1080 x 1080)
   - motorpal-logo-white.pdf      icon and name on white (1360 x 400)
   - motorpal-logo-dark.pdf       icon and name on navy (1360 x 400)
   - motorpal-mark-on-dark.pdf    the mark without its tile, on navy (1080 x 1080)
4. To add the logo to another Canva design, use Uploads > Upload files and pick an .svg file.
   SVGs stay sharp at any size and you can change their colours in Canva.

The name uses the font Plus Jakarta Sans Bold. If Canva shows a different font, select the text and pick Plus Jakarta Sans.
Colours: navy #0F172A, lime #A3E635, darker green for "Pal" on white #65A30D, white #FFFFFF.
