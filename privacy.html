Privacy policy

Last updated 7 November 2026

CarBook helps you keep your vehicle’s deadlines, fuel and service records in one place. This page explains what happens to that information. In short: it stays on your phone.

What CarBook stores

  • Vehicle details you enter, such as name, plate number, year, fuel type and odometer reading.
  • Document expiry dates and notes, such as your emission test, insurance and revenue licence.
  • Fuel fill-ups and service records, including costs, garages and notes.
  • Photos you choose to attach, such as receipts.
  • Your language choice.

Where it is stored

Everything is stored only on your device, inside the app. CarBook has no accounts and no servers that receive your records. We cannot see your data, and we do not sell or share it.

If you clear the app’s data or uninstall it, your records are deleted. Use Export on the Today tab to keep a copy. Exported files do not include photos.

What leaves your device

  • Fonts. The first time CarBook opens, it downloads its fonts from Google Fonts. Google receives your device’s internet address as part of that request, as with any website. No CarBook records are sent.
  • App updates. When online, CarBook checks for a newer version of its own files.

CarBook does not use advertising, analytics or tracking.

Camera and photos

CarBook asks to use your camera or photos only when you choose to attach a photo to a record. Photos are shrunk and kept on your device.

Children

CarBook is meant for vehicle owners and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

Future versions may add optional cloud backup or sharing. If they do, this policy will be updated before those features are released, and they will be off unless you turn them on.
