Questions

Good questions, straight answers.

Is CarBook really free?

Yes. Reminders, records, fuel tracking, the dashboard lights guide and the assistant are free for vehicle owners.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Open the app and add your vehicle. Your records are saved on your phone. Use Export in the app to keep a backup copy.

Which phones does it work on?

Any smartphone with a web browser. On Android, open it in Chrome and choose "Install app" or "Add to Home screen" to use it like a normal app. A Google Play version is on the way.

Is it in Sinhala and Tamil?

CarBook is in English today. Sinhala and Tamil versions are being written and checked by native speakers.

Does CarBook replace my mechanic?

No. CarBook explains things in plain words and suggests what to ask, but it never diagnoses. For anything that feels unsafe, especially brakes, tyres or a red warning light, see a professional.

Where do the service intervals come from?

CarBook uses common default intervals, such as an oil change every 5,000 km or 6 months, and always labels them as defaults. Your owner's manual comes first.

Can I renew my licence through CarBook?

Not directly. CarBook reminds you, shows what needs to be valid first, and keeps your renewal history. You renew through the official eRL service or your Divisional Secretariat as usual.

Is my information safe?

Your records stay on your phone and are never sent to us. Read our privacy policy for the details.
