MotorPal logo ("Four pillars")

The four segments of the dial stand for what MotorPal looks after:
reminders, service records, fuel and costs, and warranties. The needle sits in the green.

Files
- motorpal-icon.svg / .png / .pdf        the app icon (black tile)
- motorpal-logo-light.svg, motorpal-logo-white.png / .pdf   icon and name on white
- motorpal-logo-dark.svg / .png / .pdf    icon and name on black
- motorpal-mark-only.svg, motorpal-mark-on-dark.png / .pdf  the mark without its tile, for dark backgrounds

SVG files stay sharp at any size and open in Figma, Illustrator, Inkscape and Canva (Uploads).
To edit in Canva: Create a design > Import file, then pick a PDF.

Colours: black #050505, mint #00A86B, deeper mint for "Pal" on white #006E47, white #FFFFFF. The needle is white at 75% opacity.
Name font: Plus Jakarta Sans Bold (free from Google Fonts). If a tool shows another font, select the text and pick Plus Jakarta Sans.
