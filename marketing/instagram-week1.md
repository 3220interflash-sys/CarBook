Goal: useful, funny, true. No product mentions. Each post makes people think "I really don't know this about my own vehicle". Later versions in Sinhala and Tamil.

Tone: a friend who knows cars, talking to a friend who watches F1 but calls every warning light "the orange thing". Light Sri Lankan English ("machan", "aiyo", "baas"), never mocking.

Facts are kept general and hedged ("usually", "most"). Always point to the owner's manual or a mechanic. Check any figure again before posting.

## 1. The 2-second pit stop
**Format:** carousel, 5 slides

1. **F1 team:** changes all four tyres in under 2 seconds.
   **You:** haven't checked your tyres since the vehicle came home.
2. F1 crews know exactly when every part is due. Down to the lap.
3. Quick quiz: when was your last oil change?
   a) I know the date
   b) "Around the Avurudu time?"
   c) Oil change?
4. Most garages suggest roughly every 5,000 km or 6 months, whichever comes first. Your owner's manual may say different, and the manual wins.
5. You don't need a pit crew. You just need to remember.

**Caption:** Box box, machan. When was YOUR last oil change? Be honest in the comments. 👇

## 2. Your tyres have a birthday
**Format:** carousel, 4 slides

1. Your tyres have a birthday. It's written on them.
2. Look on the sidewall for a 4-digit code inside an oval, like **2319**. That's week 23 of 2019.
3. Tyres age even if they look fine. The rubber hardens and cracks over time, and that matters on wet roads. Many tyre makers suggest having them checked from about 5 years old.
4. Go find yours. If it says 2016, we need to talk.

**Caption:** Go check now. Comment your tyre's birth year. Oldest one wins nothing, but you might win a safer drive. 🛞
#TyreSafety #SriLanka #MonsoonDriving #CarTips

## 5. What's actually in a "service"?
**Format:** carousel, 5 slides

1. "Baas, full service eka karanna." But what's actually in a service?
2. **Usually in a basic service:** new engine oil and oil filter, plus checks of brakes, tyres, lights, fluids, wipers and battery.
3. **Often added at bigger services:** air filter, cabin filter, spark plugs, brake fluid, coolant. It depends on the distance and your manual.
4. **Ask before they start:** "What exactly will you do and change today, and what will it cost?"
5. **Ask when you pick it up:** "Can I see the old parts?" A good mechanic won't mind.

**Caption:** Save this for your next garage visit. Knowing the list = no surprises on the bill. 🔧
#CarService #SriLanka #MechanicTips #CarCare

## 6. The squeal is a feature, not a bug
**Format:** reel (the sound of a brake squeal, then text)

**On screen:** That squeal when you brake?
On many cars, it's a small metal tab built into the brake pad. Its job is to squeak when the pad is getting thin.
Your car is literally shouting "please".

**Caption:** Brakes are not a "next month" job. If they squeal, grind or feel different, get them checked by a professional. F1 brakes glow red-hot. Yours shouldn't sing. 🎵
#BrakeSafety #SriLanka #CarCare #DrivingTips

## 7. Tyre pressure: the number is NOT on the tyre
**Format:** carousel, 4 slides

1. Plot twist: the right tyre pressure is not the big number on the tyre.
2. The number on the tyre is the **maximum** it can take, not what your vehicle needs.
3. The right pressure is usually on a sticker inside the driver's door, or in the manual. Check when the tyres are cold.
4. Low pressure can mean more fuel used, worse grip and faster wear. Free air at the fuel shed, machan. Use it.

**Caption:** When did you last check? "When the shed uncle did it" counts, but only once. 😄
#TyrePressure #FuelSaving #SriLanka #CarTips

## 8. Monsoon mode
**Format:** carousel, 4 slides

1. **F1 wet tyres:** Pirelli says they can clear dozens of litres of water per second at race speed.
   **Your tyres in a Wellawatte flood:** 😬
2. The grooves in your tyres push water away so the rubber can grip the road. Worn grooves = sliding on water.
3. Before the monsoon: check tread depth, wiper blades, and that all your lights work.
4. In heavy rain: slow down, leave more space, and never drive into water when you can't see the road.

**Caption:** Monsoon season is coming. Save this and send it to the family WhatsApp group. 🌧️
#Monsoon #SriLanka #RainyDrive #DrivingSafety

## 9. The three-date chain
**Format:** carousel, 4 slides

1. Sri Lankan vehicle owners have a secret boss level: the three-date chain.
2. **Emission test** first. Then **insurance**. Then the **revenue licence**, which needs the other two to be valid. (Electric and hybrid vehicles don't need the emission test.)
3. Miss one, and the whole chain stalls. Usually discovered at the worst possible time.
4. Quick test: without checking, do you know all three expiry dates?

**Caption:** Be honest. How many did you know? 0, 1, 2 or 3? 👇 No judgement. (A little judgement.)
#RevenueLicence #SriLanka #VehicleOwners #EmissionTest

## 10. Water is not coolant
**Format:** single image or reel

**Text:** "Radiator ekata watura daala hari." Not quite.
Coolant does more than water: it helps stop the engine overheating, raises the boiling point and protects the metal inside from rust.
Topping up with water in an emergency is OK. Leaving it there forever is not.
And never open a hot radiator cap. Ever.

**Caption:** In 32°C traffic, your engine works harder than you do. Give it the right drink. 🥵
Ask your mechanic which coolant your vehicle uses.
#SriLanka #CarCare #EngineCare #CarTips
