## Full description (4,000 characters max)
CarBook keeps your vehicle’s deadlines, fuel and service records in one place, and helps you know what to ask your mechanic. Built for Sri Lankan owners.

NEVER MISS A DEADLINE
Your emission test, insurance and revenue licence depend on each other. CarBook shows what to book next, counts down every date, and keeps your past renewals on record. Electric and hybrid vehicles skip the emission test automatically.

TRACK FUEL AND SPENDING
Log each fill-up in seconds. CarBook works out your km per litre, your average price per litre and what you spent on fuel in the last 30 days.

KEEP EVERY SERVICE RECORD
Save oil changes, brake pads, tyres, battery and more, with the oil grade, filter, parts type, garage and cost. Attach a photo of the receipt.

SEE YOUR CAR’S CONDITION
See at a glance whether your engine oil, battery, brakes, tyres and spark plugs are fine, coming up or due, based on your own records. Default intervals only; your owner’s manual always comes first.

KNOW WHAT TO ASK YOUR MECHANIC
Before a garage visit, CarBook prepares plain questions based on your records, each showing the record it comes from. Show them to your mechanic in large text. CarBook suggests questions and never diagnoses.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account and no sign-up. Your records stay on your phone and are never sent to us. Works offline. Export a copy any time.

Sinhala and Tamil are coming soon.
